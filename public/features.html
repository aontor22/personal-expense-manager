Product overview

Understand daily expenses and stay on budget

ExpenseFlow helps you record personal spending, compare monthly trends and export reports without losing your offline workflow.

ExpenseFlow is a personal expense and budget tracker designed for everyday spending in Bangladesh and elsewhere. Record expenses, group costs by category and monitor monthly totals in a responsive browser app.

Open ExpenseFlow

What you can do

Daily expense entries and customizable categories

Record spending with a date, amount, category, payment method and optional description.

Monthly budgets and progress summaries

Set a monthly spending target and compare remaining budget with recorded costs.

Spending charts by day, category and payment method

See spending trends by day, category and payment method to better understand patterns.

IndexedDB offline-first records

Save expense entries to browser storage first, so entries remain available without a connection.

Optional per-user Supabase sync

Optionally synchronize records to an authenticated, user-scoped Supabase account when connected.

PDF, Excel, CSV and JSON backup/exports

Download PDF, Excel and CSV reports, plus JSON backups for your own records.

How it works

  1. Add an expense with amount, date and category.
  2. Set a monthly budget and review spending patterns.
  3. Export reports or back up records as needed.

Privacy and data

Expense entries are personal application data, saved in browser storage and, if enabled, synchronized with per-user Supabase access controls. This public information page does not expose any transactions.
